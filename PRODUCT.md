# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Kader posyandu (community health cadre volunteers) using the app in the field during posyandu sessions to record and review data for children, pregnant women, and mothers. They work under time pressure with many patients queued, often on phones/tablets, sometimes with weak connectivity. Priority: fast input, minimal taps, unambiguous status at a glance. A second audience (ibu / mothers) uses a separate portal to track their own pregnancy/child data.

## Product Purpose

Loka Bakka helps posyandu cadres track maternal and child health data (growth measurements, pregnancy visits, risk screening) to catch stunting and health risks early, and gives mothers visibility into their own and their children's health records. Success looks like: cadres complete accurate data entry quickly during a live session, supervisors can see clinic-wide risk at a glance, and no patient falls through the cracks between visits.

## Brand Personality

Terpercaya, jelas, hangat (trustworthy, clear, warm). Professional enough to be credible as a health tool, warm enough not to feel like cold clinical software. Confidence without coldness.

## Anti-references

Not a dense, cluttered legacy hospital/government administrative system (overwhelming tables, unclear status, buried actions). Not an overwrought modern SaaS dashboard either (unnecessary gradients, heavy animation, decorative flourish) — cadres need speed, not spectacle. Not playful/childish despite the maternal-child subject matter; users are health professionals.

## Design Principles

- Speed over ceremony: every screen used during a live posyandu session should minimize taps and reading.
- Status at a glance: risk/health status must be scannable via color and icon, not just text.
- Trustworthy plainness: clarity and restraint over decoration; warmth comes from color and tone, not gimmicks.
- Graceful with imperfect data: fields are often incomplete mid-session; the UI should not punish partial data.
- One dataset, two audiences: the same underlying records serve both the kader's working view and the ibu's read-focused view — keep data shapes consistent between them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline: sufficient color contrast for status badges (not color alone — pair with icon/text), large enough tap targets for mobile/tablet field use, readable type sizes for quick scanning under variable field lighting.
